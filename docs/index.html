IG Share

Share Instagram posts, reels and carousels with people who aren't on Instagram — as real photos and videos in WhatsApp, Telegram, or any chat app. No more login-wall links.

⬇ Download IGShare.apk

Version 1.2 · ~30 KB · Android 8.0+ · Source on GitHub

How it works

  1. In Instagram, open a post or reel → Share → pick IG Share
  2. It downloads the video — or every page of a carousel
  3. A share sheet opens with the actual files → send them anywhere

Good to know

One-time Instagram connectInstagram blocks most logged-out downloads, so you log in once on Instagram's own page inside the app. Your password never touches the app — it keeps only a session cookie, on your phone.
Private by designNo analytics, no trackers, no server of ours. Your phone talks directly to Instagram. Downloads auto-delete after 24 hours.
Full carouselsNot just the cover photo — every image and video in the post, in order.
Tiny and honest~30 KB, one permission (internet), open source under MIT. Read every line on GitHub.
